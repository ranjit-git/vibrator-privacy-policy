# Privacy Policy for Vibrator pro

**Effective Date:** July 17, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how "Vibrator pro" (the "App") handles your information. 

Our core philosophy is simple: **We do not collect, store, or share any of your personal data.** 

## 2. Data Collection and Usage
The App operates entirely offline and locally on your device. 
- **No Personal Data:** We do not collect names, emails, IP addresses, device identifiers, or any other personally identifiable information (PII).
- **No Telemetry or Analytics:** We do not use third-party analytics trackers, crash reporters, or advertising networks.
- **Local Storage Only:** Your preferences (such as your chosen vibration speed, selected pattern, auto-stop timer, and UI theme) are saved strictly locally on your device using standard platform storage (`SharedPreferences`/`NSUserDefaults`). This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any server.

## 3. Required Device Permissions
To function properly, the App requires the following device capabilities:
- **Vibration / Haptics Control:** 
  - On Android, the App requests the `android.permission.VIBRATE` permission.
  - On iOS, the App utilizes the `Core Haptics` engine.
  - *Purpose:* These are exclusively used to trigger the device's physical vibration motor to deliver the patterns you select. We do not use these APIs to track you.
- **Wakelock (Keep Screen On):**
  - The App uses standard OS wakelocks to prevent your screen from turning off while a haptic pattern is actively running. This is required to prevent the operating system from suspending the vibration engine.

## 4. Third-Party Services
The App does not integrate with any third-party services, APIs, or ad networks. Your usage is entirely private.

## 5. Children's Privacy
Because we do not collect any personal data, the App is completely safe for children and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar global regulations.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Since the App does not connect to the internet to fetch updates, any changes to this policy will be reflected in future updates to the App via the respective App Stores.
