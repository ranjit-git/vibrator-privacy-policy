# Privacy Policy for Vibrator pro

**Effective Date:** July 17, 2026  
**Last updated:** July 29, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Your privacy is important. This Privacy Policy explains how "Vibrator pro" (the "App") handles information.

The App is designed to operate primarily offline and locally on your device. However, the App now uses Firebase Crashlytics for crash reporting and diagnostics, which means limited technical information related to app crashes may be collected and transmitted to help improve app stability.

## 2. Data Collection and Usage

The App does not require you to create an account and is not designed to collect personal information such as your name, email address, contacts, photos, messages, or precise location through its core features.

The App stores certain preferences locally on your device, such as your selected vibration speed, chosen pattern, auto-stop timer, and UI theme. This locally stored data is used only to remember your settings and support app functionality.

The App also uses **Firebase Crashlytics** to collect limited technical and diagnostic information if the App crashes or encounters errors. This may include crash logs, stack traces, device model, operating system version, app version, and related diagnostic data needed to identify and fix technical issues.

## 3. Required Device Permissions

To function properly, the App requires the following device capabilities:

- **Vibration / Haptics Control**
  - On Android, the App requests the `android.permission.VIBRATE` permission.
  - On iOS, the App uses the `Core Haptics` engine.
  - **Purpose:** These are used only to trigger the device's physical vibration or haptic engine to deliver the patterns you select.

- **Wakelock (Keep Screen On)**
  - The App may use standard operating system wakelocks to help prevent the screen from turning off while a haptic pattern is actively running.
  - **Purpose:** This helps keep the active haptic session running as intended.

These permissions and capabilities are not used to track you.

## 4. Third-Party Services

The App uses **Firebase Crashlytics**, a crash reporting service provided by **Google LLC**, to monitor app crashes and improve reliability.

Firebase Crashlytics may collect information such as:

- crash logs and stack traces;
- device model and operating system version;
- app version and configuration details;
- technical identifiers associated with app installation;
- general diagnostic information related to a crash or app error.

This information is used only to detect, investigate, and fix app crashes or stability issues.

More information about Firebase privacy and security is available here:  
[https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

The App does not use advertising SDKs or analytics trackers unless disclosed in a future update to this Privacy Policy.

## 5. Data Sharing

The App does not sell your personal data and does not share personal information for advertising or marketing purposes.

Crash-related technical and diagnostic information may be processed by Firebase Crashlytics as part of the crash reporting service used by the App.

## 6. Data Storage

Your app preferences, such as vibration settings, selected patterns, timers, and theme choices, are stored locally on your device.

Crash-related information collected through Firebase Crashlytics may be transmitted to and stored on Google's servers for diagnostics and service operation.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age required by applicable law for independent consent in their jurisdiction. The App is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in a revised version of this policy with an updated effective date or last updated date.
